Curse Of The Baby Mask

A short game made with 5 developers and 4 artists in 30 hours

Don't get caught!

You’re trapped inside an asylum where something feels deeply wrong. To find a way out, you’ll need to wear a strange mask that reveals hidden symbols and pathways throughout the halls. But every time you put it on, you see something else too, a grotesque baby figure lurking just behind you, slowly closing in. The longer you keep the mask on, the closer it gets. To survive, you’ll have to solve puzzles quickly, switch between realities, and decide how much truth you dare to see before it’s too late. Piece by piece, the asylum’s disturbing history unfolds… and it may already be too late to escape.
